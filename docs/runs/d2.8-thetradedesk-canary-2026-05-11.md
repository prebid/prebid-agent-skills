## Methodology notes

- **Why thetradedesk is canary 6 (the F4 sample)**: ADR-007 F4 documents the bid-post-processing-macros pattern (substituting `${AUCTION_PRICE}` into `bid.nurl/adm/burl` after bid-type classification). thetradedesk is the spec-blessed master sample. The 46-rule corpus originally had NO rule for F4 — the SKILL ignored the pattern even though the source spec carried it as a quirk. F-new-100 is the master-sample finding; F2 Tier 3 step 3 (`d391e98`) added the scaffold + integration step into `extractBids` and introduced a new `ctx.has_bid_post_processing_macros` ctx field.
- **Why thetradedesk had the highest pre-F2 violation count (64)**: 12 exemplary fixtures × snake_case method names (12 × 2 classes = 24 `MethodName` violations) + LineLength on the longer fixture-path strings (multi-cap PascalCase + 12 scenarios push line lengths past 120 in `responseFor(...)` and `Assertions.fail(...)` invocations) drives the bulk. F2 Tier 0 retired the universal class of these; the remaining cosmetic violations were trimmed by the LineLength wrap macros.
- **Number-formatting precision asymmetry on F4**: Go's `strconv.FormatFloat 'f' -1` drops trailing zeros to ~16 significant digits; Java's `BigDecimal.toPlainString()` preserves source scale exactly. The F2 scaffold emits the Java side using `bid.getPrice().toPlainString()` — operator should be aware that bid `0.50` may emit as `0.5` (Go) vs `0.50` (Java). Documented in the SKILL prose extension landed by `d391e98`.
- **Cross-canary signal**: thetradedesk drove F-new-65 (`template-macro` endpoint resolution, the third F-new-50 family member), F-new-67 (silent Rule 11 skip on `spec_field_driver` mismatch — a SKILL prose gap), and F-new-100 (the F4 master sample). The F2 sprint absorbed F-new-65 + F-new-100; F-new-67 remains as a SKILL prose extension deferred to v1.1.0.
