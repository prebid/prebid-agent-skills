## Methodology notes

- **Why adkernelAdn is canary 3**: it is the only MVP pair where Rule 46 actually rewrites the bidder name. Surfaces both the mechanical name normalization (`adkernelAdn` → `adkerneladn`) AND the class-root preservation gap (PascalCase `AdkernelAdn` vs the mechanical `Adkerneladn`). The latter is F-new-63 (canary 3 subagent ID): a real SKILL.md §Step 4 prose gap that the canary 3 driver had to defensively patch with a `derive_class_root` helper.
- **Highest-leverage finding in the corpus**: F-new-70 (entity-mutation chains, canary 3 subagent ID; cross-canary canonical F-new-78). adkernelAdn surfaced it because its spec carries non-trivial `entity_strategies` on Imp + Site + App + Banner + Source, and the template was emitting a single `BidderUtil.defaultRequest(bidRequest, ...)` passthrough that honored none of them. Virtually every adapter with `mutates_request: true` (5/6 MVP canaries) benefits from the F-new-78 fix. F2 Tier 3 step 1 (`8ec8bb5`) was designed around this finding.
- **Per-key batching (F-new-66) remains operator hand-fill**: the F2 Tier 3 entity-mutation scaffold gives the operator a TODO at the correct Imp emit site, but the per-pubId grouping logic (`Map<Integer, List<Imp>>` bucket construction) is implementation-shape work that the template can't mechanize without a richer ctx schema. Deferred to v1.1.0.
- **Cross-canary signal**: adkernelAdn introduced F-new-96 (UnusedImports JsonNode) — a finding that NOT-DID surface on canary 1 (kobler) or canary 2 (aax) because both happened to use `ext-prebid-video-placement` bid-type resolution which DOES need JsonNode. adkernelAdn's `imp-id-correlation` chain doesn't, exposing the unconditional import. F2 Tier 0 made the import conditional on `ctx.bid_type_resolution == "ext-prebid-video-placement"`.
